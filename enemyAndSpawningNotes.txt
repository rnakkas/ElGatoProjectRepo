enemy spawner
- a singleton or child of main scene
- if player crosses a world boundary or area2d, enemy spawner will spawn enemies just outside of the camera
- enemies will run towards player's position
- enemies will stop running towards player if player is inside their area2d detection area
- if enemy is ranged, enemies will shoot at player's x position if player is inside their area2d detection area
- if player exits the area2d detection area, enemy will run towards player until player is inside the area2d detection area
