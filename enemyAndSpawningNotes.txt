ENEMY SPAWNER
- global class
- add it as a child of each level, so that it can have direct access to player node
- have a timer node, oneshot = false so it will run continuously, on timeout get player's position and spawn enemies
just outside of the camera, give them an x direction which points to the player for them to run/fly towards player
(think of it similarly to a gun, but in this case it shoots enemies at the player lol)
- will also subscribe to level trigger events when player enters an area2d to spawn enemies, these could be just outside
of camera or on screen depending on the trigger
- if enemy is ranged, eg flying drones, enemies will shoot at player's position if player is inside their area2d detection area 

+++

SPAWNING LOGIC
- have an array/list of enemies that can be spawned for each level
- choose an enemy at random from the list to spawn
- cannot spawn boss enemies

+++

The different enemy types and spawning logic

-- PRE PLACED ENEMIES, these would be placed around the level in predetermined spots, for example stationary turrets,
stationary shooters etc. If they are ranged enemies, they will shoot at the player's position.

-- SPAWNED ENEMIES, 
	--- enemies spawned by the enemy spawner on a continuous timer, on timer timeout spawner will get the player's 
	position/globalPosition, spawn the enemies and give them an x direction for them to run/fly towards the player's 
	x direction. Once they go offscreen and are a certain  distance from the player, they will despawn,or could even 
	make it so that these enemies have a hardcoded despawn time upon which they will despawn. Make sure they have a run 
	speed faster than the player so player cannot catch up with them to see them despawn. Example enemies will be melee 
	chargers, melee kamikaze, flying drones etc.
	--- enemies spawned by the enemy spawner on trigger, i.e. when the player enters an area2d, trigger the enemy spawner
	to spawn enemies, similar to above.

-- BOSS ENEMIES, similar to pre placed enemies, larger health pool, display name and health bar at bottom of screen

+++

Enemies

-- MELEE CHARGER: runs towards player, hurt player if player touches them, will run past the player in whichever direction
they are running

-- KAMIKAZE: runs towards player, explodes and hurts player on touch, explodes on death huring other enemies, if player
is too close will hurt player too

-- STATIONARY RANGED: stationary but ranged enemies, will shoot at player's position when player enters range,
e.g. turrets, ambush gunners

-- MOVING RANGED: similar movement logic to melee chargers, similar shooting logic to stationary ranged, but these will
usually be flying, e.g. drones
